Questions, answered.

Who is the Kickstart for?

B2B SaaS teams without a product marketer — or with one who's underwater — who need positioning and a pipeline-ready message fast. Typically Seed to Series C.

What if we need something that's not in the box?

Add-ons are priced up front: extra battlecards, extra landing pages, a demand-gen launch, or a win/loss program. Or roll into a retainer after week 6.

Can we start right away?

We take a limited number of Kickstarts each month so every one gets a senior team. Reserve a spot and pick any open kickoff date.

What does "fixed price" actually cover?

Everything listed in the box, two revision rounds on each deliverable, and all of our time. The only extras are add-ons you choose.

Is it refundable?

Your deposit holds your start date. If we don't hit sign-off on positioning by week 4, we keep working at no extra cost until we do.
